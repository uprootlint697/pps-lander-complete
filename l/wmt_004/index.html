Traditional Washing Machines: Breeding Grounds for Mold & Odors?

Your washing machine should be keeping your clothes fresh… but what if it’s doing the exact opposite?

Over time detergent residue, pet hair, and moisture turn your machine into a hidden breeding ground for bacteria and mold. 

That musty smell? That’s your machine begging for a deep clean. Moisture, detergent residue, and pet hair all combine to create the perfect environment for germs to thrive. Without proper cleaning, your machine could actually be making your clothes dirtier instead of cleaner.

The worst part is that the bacteria can transfer onto your clothes, spreading odors and allergens throughout your home. Fabric softeners and detergent may temporarily cover the stench, but they don’t actually clean your machine.


So now it’s up to you to find a solution… Should you scrub your washer by hand? Buy expensive cleaning solutions? Constantly run empty cycles with vinegar or baking soda?


There has to be an easier way.

Why Standard Cleaning Methods Don’t Work

Many people think that running an empty cycle with hot water or vinegar will do the trick. But in reality, these methods fail to reach deep into the machine where buildup accumulates.

Manual scrubbing might seem like an obvious solution, but it’s actually nearly impossible. All the bacteria and grime building up in your machine hide in spots you can’t reach with a normal brush. You’d actually have to take the whole machine apart to reach these areas!

Vinegar and backing soda are frequently recommended on social media, but this solution isn’t ideal either. 

This combo might make your machine smell fresh for a day, but they don’t eliminate the root problem. That grime lurking deep inside is still there, building up every time you run a cycle.


What about your washer’s self-cleaning cycle? Well, it turns out that this feature ALSO doesn’t reach the hidden areas. That means that while the drum gets clean, all the moldy gunk behind it doesn’t go anywhere.


And if you think that adding more detergent to your cycle could help, you’d be wrong. In fact, detergent only makes things WORSE! Adding that extra soap leads to more residue during the cycle, causing more buildup and even more problems.

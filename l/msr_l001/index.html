The Mold You Can See Isn’t The Real Problem

“All that bleaching is doing something, though,” my sister said.

She said that, over time, repeated bleaching can actually start wearing surfaces down.

Grout becomes brittle. Caulk gets dried out. Rubber starts cracking.

More places for mold to hide.

And the deeper it spreads, the less effective bleach becomes.

The smell comes back. You grab the bleach. The surfaces decay. The mold digs in.

Rinse. Repeat.

“Mold is like a weed,” she explained. “And all bleach does is cut the head off. It never touches the roots.”
